BabelTable support

How do I get started?

Open Settings, choose two different languages, and tap Download language models while online. Keep the app open until Ready for offline use appears, then tap Start translating and allow microphone access.

Does it work offline?

Yes, after the language pair is prepared. Speech recognition and translation then run on your device, including in airplane mode. If iOS removes the models, prepare them again while online.

My language pair isn't ready

Availability depends on your device and Apple's speech and translation models. BabelTable requires iOS 27 and a device that supports on-device speech transcription. Check your pair in Settings before traveling.

The translation went to the wrong side

Select the speaking language before each person talks, and speak one at a time. Overlapping speech and automatic speaker detection are not supported.

The microphone stopped

Backgrounding the app, a phone call, or another audio interruption pauses capture and keeps the visible text. Return to the app or tap Resume to continue. Speech during a pause is not captured.

Where are my conversations?

Tap Stop to save the conversation, then open History. Transcripts are stored only on your device; you can share or delete them there.

Contact

Email xnuapp@gmail.com with the app version, device and iOS version. Please don't include private conversations. You can also join our Discord.

中文帮助

  • 怎么开始？在“设置”里选两种不同语言，联网点“Download language models”，等出现“Ready for offline use”后点“Start translating”并允许麦克风。
  • 能离线用吗？语言模型准备好后可以，包括飞行模式。如果系统删除了模型，联网重新准备即可。
  • 语言组合不可用？取决于设备和 Apple 的语音、翻译模型，需要 iOS 27 及支持设备端语音转写的设备。出行前请在设置里先检查。
  • 译文显示到了另一边？每个人说话前先选好说话语言，并轮流说话；不支持同时说话或自动识别说话人。
  • 麦克风停了？切到后台、来电或其他音频中断会暂停并保留已有文字，回到 App 或点 Resume 继续，暂停期间的话不会被收录。
  • 对话记录在哪？点 Stop 保存后在“History”里查看，记录只保存在本机，可分享或删除。
  • 联系我们：xnuapp@gmail.com（请勿附上私人对话内容）
