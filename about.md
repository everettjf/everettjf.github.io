I’m Everett, also known as **everettjf**, a developer based in Cupertino, CA.

I write about building software, exploring how things work, and lessons from work and life. Earlier posts focus on Apple platforms, performance analysis, debugging, and reverse engineering. I also write about the tools I make and the choices behind them.

I like focused tools that solve a concrete problem. Many of my projects start with a need in my own work. My apps and games live at {% include xnu/external_link.html href=site.company_url label='AtomSprout' %}.
