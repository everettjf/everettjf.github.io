I’m Everett, also known as **everettjf**, a fullstack developer based in Cupertino, CA.

XNU is the home for my independent apps and open-source developer tools. I build software for everyday work, with a focus on Apple platforms and developer workflows.

I like focused tools that solve a concrete problem: fewer interruptions, clearer information, and a shorter path from an idea to something useful. Many of my projects start with a need in my own work.

Explore my [projects](/projects/) or browse my [technical writing](/archive). New thoughts and product progress are shared mainly in Chinese through my WeChat public account, **吃灰思考**; the [writing page](/archive#wechat) has the details. You can also follow the blog through [RSS](/feed.xml).
