I’m Everett, also known as **everettjf**, a fullstack developer based in Cupertino, CA.

This is my personal blog: a place for technical notes, open-source explorations, and things I learn while building software. My released apps and games now live at [AtomSprout](https://atomsprout.com), the product brand I am building.

I like focused tools that solve a concrete problem: fewer interruptions, clearer information, and a shorter path from an idea to something useful. Many of my projects start with a need in my own work.

Explore my [open-source projects](/projects/) or browse my [technical writing](/archive). New thoughts and product progress are shared mainly in Chinese through my WeChat public account, **吃灰思考**; the [writing page](/archive#wechat) has the details. You can also follow the blog through [RSS](/feed.xml).
