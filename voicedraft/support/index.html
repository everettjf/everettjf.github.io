support

VoiceDraft support

Record and create

Record or import audio, then choose an action: summarize, research with sources, or prepare the work. You can edit these prompts or add your own under Manage Actions. Each result is one Markdown draft you can preview, edit, copy, or share as a .md file.

Web research

Research needs an external provider configured for Responses web search. VoiceDraft asks before sending the transcript and selected prompt to that provider. It does not send the original audio for research.

Processing problems

Check microphone permission and Apple Intelligence availability. An interrupted task keeps its recording and can be retried. External-provider settings are optional and separate from the Apple workflow.

Contact

Email xnuapp@gmail.com with the app version, device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce. Do not send API keys, recordings, or private transcripts unless needed and you choose to share them.
