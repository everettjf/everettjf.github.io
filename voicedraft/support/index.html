support

VoiceDraft support

Record and create

Record or import audio, open its transcript, then choose a plan, presentation outline, document, or short book. Review the result and edit sections before exporting.

Processing problems

Check microphone permission and Apple Intelligence availability. An interrupted task keeps its recording and can be retried. External-provider settings are optional and separate from the Apple workflow.

Contact

Email xnuapp@gmail.com with the app version, device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce. Do not send API keys, recordings, or private transcripts unless needed and you choose to share them.
