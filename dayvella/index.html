Dayvella

iOS · iPadOS

Keep the dates worth remembering close.

Dayvella (formerly CountMyDays) is for the dates worth remembering and the dates worth waiting for. Track birthdays, anniversaries, trips, and personal milestones with cards and widgets. Entries are stored locally for offline access and automatically synchronized through your iCloud account.

Download on the App Store Join Discord Feedback

features

Count up or down

Track how long since something happened, or how long until something will.

Gradient cards

Pick a color and a style. Pin favorites to the top.

Offline access + iCloud

Use your entries offline. Changes sync automatically through your iCloud account when available.

Import & export

Move your cards between devices via a JSON file you own.

Widgets

Surface the dates you care about on the home screen.

Free

No subscriptions. No upsells.
