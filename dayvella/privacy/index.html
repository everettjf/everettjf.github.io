Privacy policy

Dayvella

Dates and settings

Dayvella stores your entries and settings on your device. When iCloud is available, entries synchronize through Apple's iCloud service under your Apple account. The developer does not operate a server for your entries or receive their contents.

Analytics and advertising

Dayvella has no advertising or in-app analytics SDK. The app does not send usage data or crash reports to a developer-operated service. Apple may handle device diagnostics according to your Apple settings.

Import and export

You choose when to import or export a JSON backup and where to save or share it. Files you export are under your control.

Contact

If you email us for support, we receive the message and your email address so we can reply. For privacy questions, email xnuapp@gmail.com. This policy was updated September 27, 2026.
