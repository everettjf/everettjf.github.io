Palmino support

Getting started

Open More → Settings, add your own compatible model provider and API key, choose an active model, and save. Provider keys remain in Keychain. Remote AI features require a compatible provider account; usage charges may apply. Voice Conversation has its own OpenAI key setting.

Tools and connections

Enable only the Apple capabilities, MCP servers, and optional Linux tools you intend to use. Review confirmation prompts for sensitive actions and check the activity history when diagnosing a tool result.

Where are my conversations stored?

Starting with version 1.0.26 build 38, conversations and attachments stay in local app storage; automatic iCloud sync and automatic app-data backup are unavailable to protect health information. Keep any backups you need through explicit exports to a destination you trust. Updating does not remove older cloud copies or backups.

How do Apple Health permissions work?

Open More → Settings → Tool Permissions to control Apple Health reading and writing separately. System HealthKit authorization is separate from the model-sharing confirmation shown for each health action in build 38 and later. Choose Don't Allow to stop that action. If privacy protection cannot be verified, unlock your device and use Retry Privacy Protection in the Conversation Storage section before trying again. You can revoke access in Apple Health or iOS Settings.

How do I delete data or stop sharing?

Delete conversations from the conversation list and manage separately saved documents, recordings, workspace files, and exports in their respective locations. Disable tool permissions, remove unwanted connections, or remove saved provider keys and save Settings to stop future requests. Revoke a key with its provider if you want to invalidate it. Deleting local content does not remove data already sent to a provider, earlier cloud copies, or Apple Health records.

Contact

Email xnuapp@gmail.com with the app version, device, iOS version, and steps to reproduce. Do not send provider keys, personal health records, or private conversations.
